# Chrome Web Store Listing - Faucet Pro

## 📝 Description
**Faucet Pro: The Most Intuitive Faucet Automation Tool**

Tired of manually clicking on faucets all day? Faucet Pro is a professional-grade browser extension designed to automate your favorite cryptocurrency faucets with a single click. Our mission is to provide a clean, secure, and highly efficient "set-and-forget" experience.

**✨ Why Choose Faucet Pro?**
*   **Simple & Beautiful**: A modern, glassmorphic dashboard that makes automation a breeze.
*   **Smart Scheduling**: Automatically claims from your favorite sites at optimal intervals.
*   **Human-Like Interaction**: Built-in anti-detection technology with randomized delays and "Long Breaks" (65-80 minutes) to keep your accounts safe.
*   **Auto-Withdrawals**: Set your payout threshold and let the bot handle the rest.
*   **Real-Time Alerts**: Connect your Telegram bot to get instant notifications on every successful claim and withdrawal.

**🌐 Supported Faucet Networks:**
*   Litepick (LTC)
*   Dogepick (DOGE)
*   Solpick (SOL)
*   Bnbpick (BNB)
*   Tronpick (TRX)
*   Polpick (POL/MATIC)

**🛡️ Privacy First:**
*   **100% Local**: All your settings and wallet addresses are stored only on your computer.
*   **Encrypted Storage**: Your configuration data is safely encrypted within the extension.
*   **No Data Collection**: We never track your activity or see your balances.

**🚀 Version 2.6.0 - Latest Enhancements:**
*   🎉 **New Welcome Wizard**: A 3-step setup process to get you started in seconds.
*   ⚡ **Improved Anti-Detection**: Optimized native click emulation for better stability.
*   📊 **Rebranded Dashboard**: Cleaned up layout with focused "Bot Status" monitoring.
*   🔒 **Bug Fixes**: Improved handling of withdrawal thresholds and Telegram alert reliability.
